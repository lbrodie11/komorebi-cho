# 17 — Launch (free)

## Context (shared by every prompt)
You are working on **Komorebi-chō**, a free, web-first, cozy living-diorama game: a fictional Yanaka-style Tokyo shitamachi block on a walnut plinth. It's rendered in three.js (`WebGLRenderer`, r187+) at low resolution through a palette + Bayer-dither post pass, so it reads as pixel art. Residents follow schedules and a utility AI, notice what the player places, and adapt their routines. **All art and audio are generated by code.** No purchased or commissioned assets, no image or audio files.

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before touching code. Respect the non-negotiables there:
- code-only art
- the palette is law
- no fail states
- never teleport visible residents
- edits are transactions
- determinism via named RNG streams
- respectful, fictional specificity
- a 60 fps budget

Working method:
- Plan first. List the files you will touch and why.
- Implement in small steps. After each one, run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`.
- Run the dev server and **look**: capture screenshots (Playwright or the browser) at hours 6, 12, 18 and 22, plus rain. Compare them to the screenshots from before your change.
- New logic in `src/sim`, `src/core` or `src/audio/theory.ts` needs unit tests.
- Don't add a dependency unless it removes more complexity than it adds. Say why.
- Finish with a short report:
  - what changed
  - screenshots
  - known gaps
  - which acceptance criteria you verified, and how

---

## Goal
A calm, confident launch that finds the cozy and pixel-art audiences.

## Tasks
1. **Landing page:**
   - The game itself is the hero: an embedded live view with sound off by default.
   - One-line hook: "Build a tiny neighbourhood. Watch it notice."
   - A play button, a share link and a short "made entirely in code" note.
   - Open Graph images generated by the photo pipeline.
2. **Trailer capture.**
   - Deterministic camera paths driven by a script (dawn → bench placed → resident discovers it → rain → night lanterns → seasons montage).
   - Audio from the offline soundtrack render.
   - Export a 30 s and a 60 s cut, and include an 8 s seamless loop for social.
3. **Press kit:** description, fact sheet, 10 screenshots, GIFs, the logo (code-rendered wordmark), the technique write-up ("How every pixel is code"), and contact.
4. **Devlog plan:** 6 short posts, one per system (pixel pass, generative piano, residents who notice, the shrine island, seasons, the soundscape). Cross-post to itch.io, Bluesky, Mastodon and the three.js forum showcase.
5. **Feedback loop.** An in-game "send feedback" link (a mailto or a form you control), an issue template, and a public roadmap.
6. **Open source decision.** Decide whether to open the repo. The scaffold is MIT and fits your open-source developer-tools profile; the decision is yours. Document it in `docs/LAUNCH.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
- The landing page loads in under 2 s on 4G and plays on mobile.
- The trailer conveys the hook with the sound off, via captions.

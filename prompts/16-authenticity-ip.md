# 16 — Authenticity & IP review

## Context (shared by every prompt)
You are working on **Komorebi-chō**, a free, web-first, cozy living-diorama game: a fictional Yanaka-style Tokyo shitamachi block on a walnut plinth. It's rendered in three.js (`WebGLRenderer`, r187+) at low resolution through a palette + Bayer-dither post pass, so it reads as pixel art. Residents follow schedules and a utility AI, notice what the player places, and adapt their routines. **All art and audio are generated by code.** No purchased or commissioned assets, no image or audio files.

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before touching code. Respect the non-negotiables there:
- code-only art
- the palette is law
- no fail states
- never teleport visible residents
- edits are transactions
- determinism via named RNG streams
- respectful, fictional specificity
- a 60 fps budget

Working method:
- Plan first. List the files you will touch and why.
- Implement in small steps. After each one, run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`.
- Run the dev server and **look**: capture screenshots (Playwright or the browser) at hours 6, 12, 18 and 22, plus rain. Compare them to the screenshots from before your change.
- New logic in `src/sim`, `src/core` or `src/audio/theory.ts` needs unit tests.
- Don't add a dependency unless it removes more complexity than it adds. Say why.
- Finish with a short report:
  - what changed
  - screenshots
  - known gaps
  - which acceptance criteria you verified, and how

## Goal
Specific without being appropriative or infringing. The place should feel like Tokyo to someone from Tokyo, and belong to no one else's brand.

## Tasks
1. **Cultural pass**, using a checklist in `docs/CULTURE.md`:
   - **Shrines:** torii orientation, kitsune pairs at inari shrines, Benzaiten on water.
   - **Behaviour:** residents bow and clap appropriately, nobody sits on or climbs shrines, and cats may sit *near* them. Removing the islet shrine or the temple requires a deliberate confirm and is framed respectfully, or isn't allowed at all (decide and document).
   - **Sento:** noren colours and the chimney.
   - **Mail and rubbish:** pillar-box red for post, and garbage nets, which are a real and charming shitamachi detail.
2. **Japanese text.**
   - All signage uses invented shop names and crests.
   - Have a native speaker review the kana and kanji glyphs and names, and record their sign-off.
   - Avoid nonsense kanji.
3. **IP sweep:**
   - no JR or other rail liveries (we removed the train)
   - no real beverage or konbini brands on vending machines
   - no real shop names
   - no compositions copied from Ghibli, Shinkai or other studios' backgrounds (the influences are colour and value, not layouts)
   - the "Quack-like" look is a technique, so don't copy their island composition or UI
4. **Fonts and licenses.** Ship the OFL licenses. Credits list tools and inspirations (Yanaka, Sudo-kōen as a public park; the "100 Soundscapes of Japan" as an idea).
5. **Sensitivity reader.** Find a Japanese reader to review personas, blurbs and diaries for stereotypes, and apply their notes.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every checklist item is resolved, with the reviewer's notes linked.
- Zero real brands or names appear in-game (grep the strings plus a manual visual pass).

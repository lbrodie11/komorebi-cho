## Goal
Move buildings from hand-written code (`src/world/buildings.ts`) to **data + grammar**, so buildings can be placed, resized and re-evaluated, and the seams between neighbours generate themselves.

## Design (from research)
- **Archetype families:**
  - machiya shop-house, Showa shop-house
  - kyosho concrete micro-home, walk-up apāto
  - corner commercial, sento, kissaten
  - pocket shrine, temple hall
- **Semantic facade zones:** `foundation → shopfront | entry → wall bays → upper windows → balcony/eaves → roof → attachments → signage → weathering`.
- **Resizing re-evaluates the grammar.** It recomputes bay count, window spacing, awning length, gutter runs and sign placement. It never scales a mesh.
- **Priority layers:** structure > circulation > roof/eaves > awnings > signage > wires > props. A lower layer yields: eaves trim to the neighbour's plane minus 2 cm, and signs slide or drop to an A-frame.
- **Determinism:** each building's seed is `hash(parcelSeed, archetype, revision)`. Neighbours re-evaluate only their **seam** components.
- **Setback rule** (Building Standards Act 42-2): a "rebuilt" parcel facing a lane under 4 m steps back, which gives the staggered frontages of real shitamachi.

## Tasks

   - `archetypes/*.ts`, one per family, as data plus small rule functions
2. Port every existing building to a spec and delete the hand code. Pixel-diff screenshots must match the old ones within reason (document any intended differences).
3. **Seam grammar.** Classify gaps between buildings with `classifyGap(a, b)`:
   - under 0.6 m: utility gap (pipes, condensers)
   - 0.6–1.1 m: service passage (crates, drain)
   - 1.1–2.0 m: yokocho candidate (lantern string, stools, paving)
   - facing food uses: shared awning
   - backs facing: laundry and privacy screens

   Seam outputs are keyed by `(edge.seed, ruleId)`.
4. **Utility wires.** Poles get sockets (power high / telecom mid / service low). Wires are a minimum spanning tree over poles plus building drop sockets, with a max span of about 30 m, and catenary spans. Moving a pole re-solves only its spans and inserts an auxiliary anchor if a roof intersects. Ugly-but-plausible is good.
5. **Tests:**
   - Seam stability: 1,000 random edits leave unrelated buildings' geometry hash unchanged.
   - Grammar determinism.
   - No geometry exceeds the plinth clip volume.

## Acceptance criteria

- Placing two shop-houses 1.4 m apart produces a yokocho with lanterns. At 0.5 m apart it produces pipes and AC units.
- Resizing a shop-house from 4 m to 6 m frontage adds a bay and moves the sign. Nothing stretches.

# 18 — Award polish

## Context (shared by every prompt)
You are working on **Komorebi-chō**, a free, web-first, cozy living-diorama game: a fictional Yanaka-style Tokyo shitamachi block on a walnut plinth. It's rendered in three.js (`WebGLRenderer`, r187+) at low resolution through a palette + Bayer-dither post pass, so it reads as pixel art. Residents follow schedules and a utility AI, notice what the player places, and adapt their routines. **All art and audio are generated by code.** No purchased or commissioned assets, no image or audio files.

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before touching code. Respect the non-negotiables there:
- code-only art
- the palette is law
- no fail states
- never teleport visible residents
- edits are transactions
- determinism via named RNG streams
- respectful, fictional specificity
- a 60 fps budget

Working method:
- Plan first. List the files you will touch and why.
- Implement in small steps. After each one, run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`.
- Run the dev server and **look**: capture screenshots (Playwright or the browser) at hours 6, 12, 18 and 22, plus rain. Compare them to the screenshots from before your change.
- New logic in `src/sim`, `src/core` or `src/audio/theory.ts` needs unit tests.
- Don't add a dependency unless it removes more complexity than it adds. Say why.
- Finish with a short report:
  - what changed
  - screenshots
  - known gaps
  - which acceptance criteria you verified, and how

---

## Goal
The last 10% that people remember.

## Tasks
1. **The first 90 seconds, scripted beat by beat.** Write it down, then make it true:
   - 0–10 s: the island fades up, golden hour, sound prompt
   - 10–30 s: the eye lands on the kissaten window, a cat stretches
   - 30–60 s: the bench hint, placement pop, a resident notices
   - 60–90 s: the bell rings, lamps flicker on one by one down the lane

   Time it with three strangers.
2. **Juice audit.** List every animation and sound and rate each one. Cut what's noise and amplify what carries meaning.
3. **Consistency audit:**
   - palette usage (no stray colours)
   - outline weights
   - icon pixel grid
   - copy tone (sentence case, warm, unvarnished)
   - timing curves (one easing family)
4. **"Things to discover" list.** At least 25 small secrets, all deterministic and code-only. For example:
   - the cat that sits on the islet at full moon
   - the bookseller reading the same book for a week
   - Genzo's bell timing
   - a lost glove on the bridge railing in winter
   - the kissaten radio changing songs at 3 pm
5. **Bug bash.** Two hours with five people. Fix every crash, every visual pop and every confusing moment.
6. **Performance last pass** on the lowest-spec target device.
7. **Credits screen** listing everyone and every inspiration.

## Acceptance criteria
- 3 of 3 strangers say "oh!" at the first resident reaction, without prompting.
- Median session in the playtest is over 20 minutes.
- Zero known crashes, and zero visible pops in the first hour.

## Goal
Let players compose the neighbourhood itself (place, move, resize, rotate buildings and props) with frictionless, reversible, transactional edits. Still no budgets, no fail states, no timers.

## Tasks
1. **Parcel grid.** Footprints are quantised internally to 0.25 m, with optional 0.5 m snapping. Free rotation with magnetic snaps at 0/15/30/45° and to nearby facades. Every object owns three footprints:
   - visual bounds
   - placement clearance
   - navigation/interaction bounds

   Eaves may overlap visually without blocking walking.
2. **Transactions.** `src/edit/transaction.ts` runs preview → validate → commit → resolve:
   - Preview runs the grammar on a copy-on-write shadow and ghosts the result.
   - Validate covers hard rules only (overlap, off-plinth). Soft issues show as hints and never block.
   - Commit locks the affected smart objects, classifies agents in the region, invalidates nav, rebuilds seams, emits `WorldEditEvent`, and pushes the inverse command.
   - Resolve sends displaced agents to recovery anchors (doors, street nodes). Nobody teleports while visible.
3. **Undo/redo.** Use a command stack with small serialisable commands (`MoveBuilding`, `Resize`, `PlaceProp`...). Commands store seeds, so redo regenerates identical geometry. The stack doubles as the replay log for prompt 13.
4. **Edge cases**, each needing a test or a scripted scenario:
   - Placing a building on residents: they shuffle aside over 0.3–0.6 s.
   - Deleting an occupied shop: a 1.5 s eviction through the door, then removal.
   - Deleting stairs or sealing an alley: islands get a dotted "unreachable" hint, and trapped agents walk to the nearest anchor or fade while occluded.
   - Mid-animation edits (sitting, in a doorway): the action proxy persists until a safe marker.
   - Rapid repeated edits: debounce nav rebuilds and keep the sim responsive.
5. **Catalogue.** Grow placeables to 30+. Each one is defined as data (mesh builder, footprint, affordances, novelty, season variants). Ideas:
   - furniture: benches, planters, stone lanterns, bicycle rack, mailbox, bus stop
   - signage and shrine pieces: noren sets, sandwich boards, fox statues, ema board
   - street life: laundry pole, potted bonsai, wind chime (furin, with sound), festival lantern string, tanuki statue, beer crates, vending variants, cat house, small vegetable planter
6. **UI.** Hotbar with categories, a ghost with a validity ring and a reason tooltip ("on the lane", "too close to the pond"), R/Shift+R rotation, Alt to disable snapping, multi-select later.

## Acceptance criteria
- **The success test from the research:** move the kissaten across the street, then watch 3 minutes. Routines visibly reorganise, and 4 of 5 testers can say who changed what.
- 500 scripted random edits (fuzz) produce zero stuck agents, zero visible teleports, and zero exceptions.
- Undo/redo of 50 mixed edits returns to a byte-identical world hash.

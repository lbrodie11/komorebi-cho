# 13 — Save, share & photo mode

## Context (shared by every prompt)
You are working on **Komorebi-chō**, a free, web-first, cozy living-diorama game: a fictional Yanaka-style Tokyo shitamachi block on a walnut plinth. It's rendered in three.js (`WebGLRenderer`, r187+) at low resolution through a palette + Bayer-dither post pass, so it reads as pixel art. Residents follow schedules and a utility AI, notice what the player places, and adapt their routines. **All art and audio are generated by code.** No purchased or commissioned assets, no image or audio files.

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before touching code. Respect the non-negotiables there:
- code-only art
- the palette is law
- no fail states
- never teleport visible residents
- edits are transactions
- determinism via named RNG streams
- respectful, fictional specificity
- a 60 fps budget

Working method:
- Plan first. List the files you will touch and why.
- Implement in small steps. After each one, run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`.
- Run the dev server and **look**: capture screenshots (Playwright or the browser) at hours 6, 12, 18 and 22, plus rain. Compare them to the screenshots from before your change.
- New logic in `src/sim`, `src/core` or `src/audio/theory.ts` needs unit tests.
- Don't add a dependency unless it removes more complexity than it adds. Say why.
- Finish with a short report:
  - what changed
  - screenshots
  - known gaps
  - which acceptance criteria you verified, and how

## Goal
Let people keep, share and show their neighbourhoods with no servers.

## Tasks
1. **Saves.**
   - Keep the versioned schema with migrations (`src/save/storage.ts`) and add tests for every migration.
   - Atomic writes: write a temp key, then swap it in. Keep 3 rolling backups.
   - Call `navigator.storage.persist()` when available.
   - Export and import a `.komorebi` file (JSON plus a deflate of the command log).
2. **Share codes.**
   - The world is `seed + command log`: compress with deflate, then base64url, then put it in `#town-<code>`.
   - Opening a link loads that town in a read-only "visit" mode, with a "make it mine" button.
   - Keep codes under about 2 KB for typical towns, and show the size.
3. **Photo mode v2.**
   - Free time scrub, weather and season pickers, and a hidden-UI toggle.
   - A focus pull: a slight dither-blur outside a radius, drawn in the pixel pass.
   - Frames: the plinth in a vignette and a polaroid border with date and town name in glyph font.
   - Export at 4× or 8× nearest-neighbour upscale as PNG.
   - A "time-lapse" export: 24 frames across a day as an animated PNG or GIF via a small encoder written in code (no dependency if feasible).
   - An optional "8-bit print" filter (a reduced palette).
4. **Privacy.** No analytics by default. If any telemetry is added later, it's opt-in, anonymous, and documented in `PRIVACY.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
- Round trips are byte-identical: export → import, and share code → visit → make it mine.
- A share link for a town with 40 edits is under 2 KB.
- Photo export works in Chrome, Firefox and Safari. The time-lapse is under 10 MB.

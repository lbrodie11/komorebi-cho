# 15 — QA & testing

## Context (shared by every prompt)
You are working on **Komorebi-chō**, a free, web-first, cozy living-diorama game: a fictional Yanaka-style Tokyo shitamachi block on a walnut plinth. It's rendered in three.js (`WebGLRenderer`, r187+) at low resolution through a palette + Bayer-dither post pass, so it reads as pixel art. Residents follow schedules and a utility AI, notice what the player places, and adapt their routines. **All art and audio are generated by code.** No purchased or commissioned assets, no image or audio files.

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before touching code. Respect the non-negotiables there:
- code-only art
- the palette is law
- no fail states
- never teleport visible residents
- edits are transactions
- determinism via named RNG streams
- respectful, fictional specificity
- a 60 fps budget

Working method:
- Plan first. List the files you will touch and why.
- Implement in small steps. After each one, run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`.
- Run the dev server and **look**: capture screenshots (Playwright or the browser) at hours 6, 12, 18 and 22, plus rain. Compare them to the screenshots from before your change.
- New logic in `src/sim`, `src/core` or `src/audio/theory.ts` needs unit tests.
- Don't add a dependency unless it removes more complexity than it adds. Say why.
- Finish with a short report:
  - what changed
  - screenshots
  - known gaps
  - which acceptance criteria you verified, and how

---

## Goal
Confidence to change anything without fear.

## Tasks
1. **Visual regression.** Playwright drives the seed URL from prompt 02 across 4 hours × 2 weathers × 4 seasons × 2 zooms. Use a pixel-diff threshold per shot, with the GPU in CI set to SwiftShader. Keep the baselines in the repo, and document how to update them deliberately.
2. **Determinism replay.** Record a session (inputs and commands), replay it headless, and compare world hashes and resident positions every simulated minute. Run it in CI.
3. **Fuzzers:**
   - **Edit fuzzer:** random place, move and remove at 10× speed for 10 simulated days. Assert no stuck agents, no exceptions, and no leaks.
   - **Input fuzzer:** random UI clicks and keys.
4. **Sim invariants**, checked every tick in dev builds:
   - no visible agent inside an obstacle
   - no agent in two reservations
   - attention slots no higher than the cap
   - every hidden agent has a valid `hiddenAt`
5. **Cross-browser matrix:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari 26+ (macOS/iOS), Android Chrome. Record the results in `docs/QA.md`.
6. **Accessibility:** run axe in CI, plus a manual screen-reader pass (VoiceOver and NVDA) over the menus.
7. **Bug-bash template** and a triage guide.

8. **Keep the UI audit.** `scripts/ui-audit.mjs` (from the 2026-11-11 button audit) clicks every control and checks the real game state. Run it in CI against `vite preview`, and extend it whenever a control is added.
9. **Real-device pass**, recorded in `docs/QA.md`: iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, an Intel/AMD integrated-GPU Windows laptop, a 4K monitor, macOS Safari. On each: pinch, twist, tap-twice placement, the More fold, Sound on then lock and unlock the phone, and frame rate at the default zoom at 21:00 in rain.

10. **Camera checks.** Screenshots at 0.8×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× and 3×, same rotation, at noon and at night in rain, in all four looks: look for shimmer while panning (Chunky at 0.8× is the riskiest), edge quality and silhouettes. Also test: a Mac trackpad pinch (arrives as a ctrl+wheel event in Chrome and Firefox; Safari uses its own gesture events), momentum scrolling, a high-refresh display, an ultrawide and a portrait phone (Fit gives 0.8× centred, by design), and zooming while a placement preview is up.

11. **Noticing Engine checks** (prompt 09b), in CI:
    - the attribution and metamorphic tests;
    - the resident-diversity index (1 − Σp²) over a 10-day soak with random edits;
    - the optional verified badge's divergence rate, logged.
12. **Browser tools pinned in CI.** Install Playwright and axe-core at exact versions in the CI job, in one `npm i --no-save` step as locally, so the UI audit and the axe scan can't quietly skip.
13. **WCAG 2.2, beyond what axe sees:**
    - every drag has a non-drag alternative (2.5.7 Dragging Movements: moving a shop or a bench by buttons or keys);
    - targets are at least 24×24 CSS px (2.5.8);
    - nothing is told by color alone (the routine ring, and the bond rungs on the card, also have a shape or words).

## Acceptance criteria
- CI runs unit, visual, replay and fuzz suites in under 15 minutes, and all are green.
- A known bug injected into nav, AI or post is caught by at least one suite (show this).

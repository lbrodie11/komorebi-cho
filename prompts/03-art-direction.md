# 03 — Art direction pass

## Context (shared by every prompt)
You are working on **Komorebi-chō**, a free, web-first, cozy living-diorama game: a fictional Yanaka-style Tokyo shitamachi block on a walnut plinth. It's rendered in three.js (`WebGLRenderer`, r187+) at low resolution through a palette + Bayer-dither post pass, so it reads as pixel art. Residents follow schedules and a utility AI, notice what the player places, and adapt their routines. **All art and audio are generated by code.** No purchased or commissioned assets, no image or audio files.

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before touching code. Respect the non-negotiables there:
- code-only art
- the palette is law
- no fail states
- never teleport visible residents
- edits are transactions
- determinism via named RNG streams
- respectful, fictional specificity
- a 60 fps budget

Working method:
- Plan first. List the files you will touch and why.
- Implement in small steps. After each one, run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`.
- Run the dev server and **look**: capture screenshots (Playwright or the browser) at hours 6, 12, 18 and 22, plus rain. Compare them to the screenshots from before your change.
- New logic in `src/sim`, `src/core` or `src/audio/theory.ts` needs unit tests.
- Don't add a dependency unless it removes more complexity than it adds. Say why.
- Finish with a short report:
  - what changed
  - screenshots
  - known gaps
  - which acceptance criteria you verified, and how

## Goal
Make every frame look hand-painted in pixels: causal weathering, legible miniature signage, living foliage, believable water. All in code.

## Principles
- **Value structure first, colour second.** Squint at screenshots: buildings must separate from the ground and sky at every hour.
- **One culturally loud prop for every three quiet ones** (lantern : meter box, gutter, crate).
- **Weathering is causal.** Grime sits under eaves, rust under fasteners, streaks under AC drain lines, darkening at the base of walls. Moving a building moves its stains.

## Tasks
1. **Weathering attributes.** Add per-vertex attributes `ao`, `upFacing`, `dripMask` and `age` to building geometry in `kit.ts`. Use a small `onBeforeCompile` patch on `MeshStandardMaterial` to darken and desaturate:
   - base grime: low `ao` plus world y near the ground
   - drip streaks: world-space noise stretched in y, below eave lines
   - moss on north faces of the stone wall
   - wetness darkening during rain
2. **Signage glyph atlas.** Design a 5×5 / 7×7 bitmap glyph set: a small set of kana, plus pictograms for ramen, coffee, flowers, books, bath and bread. Use it for shop signs, noren crests, the shotengai arch and the plinth plaque, drawn to canvas textures at native pixel size (no font files). Use only **invented** shop names and get them checked in prompt 16.
3. **Foliage.**
   - Vertex-shader wind sway on tree blobs (amplitude follows weather wind; zero with reduced motion).
   - Subtle colour variation per blob.
   - Falling leaves in autumn spawn from tree canopies, not from the sky.
4. **Water.**
   - An animated ripple pattern on the pond (scrolling noise in the water material's emissive or normal).
   - A sky-tinted fresnel highlight.
   - Lantern reflections faked by sampling a mirrored copy of the low-res frame inside a pond mask in the post pass.
5. **Lighting keys.** Tune `src/render/sky.ts` keyframes against reference moods:
   - golden hour (17:30)
   - blue hour (19:15): the kissaten window must be the warmest thing in frame
   - rainy evening
   - snowy morning

   Document the intent of each key in comments.
6. **Plinth.**
   - A bevelled walnut edge with a highlight line.
   - A better cutaway: soil strata, a gas pipe, a water main and a fibre conduit, all labelled in tiny glyphs.
   - The brass plaque using the new glyph atlas.
7. **Props density pass.** Add quiet props from the research list:
   - meter boxes, road mirrors, gutters, hose reels, umbrella stands, milk crates, garbage nets
   - faded stickers on poles, potted plants on every doorstep (Yanaka), a mailbox

## Acceptance criteria
- A blind screenshot test: 5 of 7 people say it "looks like a miniature" and want to zoom in.
- At the default view the eye goes kissaten → shrine island → chimney (describe how you checked this).
- No pure black and no pure white pixels except stars and glow cores.

## Verify
- Shot list: 4 times × 2 weathers × 4 seasons = 32 screenshots in the PR, as a contact sheet.
